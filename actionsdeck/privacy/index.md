**Effective date: November 3, 2026**

ActionsDeck is built by Siddharth Patel. This policy explains how the ActionsDeck app
handles information when you use it.

## Information collection

ActionsDeck does not operate an account service or analytics backend. It does not include
advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not sell, rent or use your data for
tracking or advertising.

## GitHub data

ActionsDeck connects directly from your device to GitHub's APIs at your request. GitHub
may return account, repository, workflow, run, log and artifact information needed to
provide the app's features. GitHub's handling of that information is governed by
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Your GitHub credential is stored in Apple's Keychain on your device. It is sent to GitHub
when required to perform the actions you request. ActionsDeck does not send the credential
to a developer-operated server.

## Information stored on your device

ActionsDeck may store repository, workflow and run metadata, logs, downloaded artifacts,
preferences and activity read state on your device so the app can work efficiently and
keep previously loaded information available offline. You can clear downloaded content
from Settings. Signing out removes the credential and account-bound local data.

Apple may process information as part of operating the App Store, iCloud, Keychain,
device diagnostics or other system services you enable. Apple's handling of that
information is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Data retention and security

Local cached data is subject to the app's storage and retention controls. ActionsDeck uses
Apple platform protections such as Keychain and app-container isolation, but no method of
storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Children

ActionsDeck is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13. The app does
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when ActionsDeck's data practices change. The effective date at
the top will be revised when a material update is published.
